# nitelos: brand and motion system

The single source of direction for the logo, the films, the static kit and the
README visuals. Brand canon written 2026-10-20 at the owner's request ("create
all the branding"); every choice below carries its one-line reason (antislop
R-31). Applies to English-language assets.

## Design Read

Reading this as: a launch film and identity for developers who build AI agents,
in an editorial, type-led technical language, dial **ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 3**.

- ENERGY 2: a developer SDK earns confidence, not spectacle.
- RHYTHM 2: scenes share one grid and one camera, with deliberate breaks at the
  two verdicts.
- MOTION 3: the films are motion pieces; choreography carries the explanation.

## Concept card

```
Idea:        An outcome is only reached when evidence completes it.
Metaphor:    telos (Greek: the end, the outcome). A stroke that stops short
             until a piece of evidence lands and finishes it.
Signature:   the evidence square. It travels along the JSON pointer trace,
             docks under the n, and the word "nitelos" is complete.
Emotion:     first 3 seconds: a confident claim that feels unearned.
Motion verb: trace, then stop. Lines draw along a path and stop dead at their
             end; nothing bounces, nothing floats.
Screenshot:  the verdict frame: decision: failure, with the pointer line running
             from /validation_error to the observed value true.
```

Concepts considered (awwwards Phase 1), scored originality / fit / feasibility /
portability (lower portability is better):

| | A. The pointer (`/` resolves to a value) | B. The end stop (telos as a bar) | C. The n that evidence completes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scores | 3 / 4 / 5 / 3 | 4 / 3 / 5 / 3 | 4 / 5 / 4 / 1 |

Picked C: it is the brand initial, it states the tagline as a shape, and it
cannot be moved to another product. Stolen from A: the pointer trace becomes the
connector line in the films.

## Logo

Generated by `tools/brand.py` from Archivo (wdth 125, wght 700) as pure outlines.

- **Wordmark** (primary): `nitelos`, lowercase. The n's right leg stops one gap
  short of the baseline; the evidence square (one stem wide, S x S) completes it.
- **Mark**: that n alone. **Tile**: the mark on a ground tile, rounded 18 %, for
  favicons and avatars.
- One accent per logo: the square is the only colored element.
- Clear space: one stem (S) on every side. Minimum size: mark 16 px, wordmark
  72 px wide.
- Files: `assets/svg/nitelos-{wordmark,mark,tile}-{dark,light}.svg`, copied to
  `docs/assets/brand/`.

## Palette

Dark is the primary theme. Reason: it is a developer tool whose evidence is code
and terminal output, and the films play on dark social feeds. Light variants
exist for the README on GitHub light mode.

| Role | Dark | Light | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground | `#101318` | `#FFFFFF` | carbon, not pure black: keeps the ivory soft on screens |
| panel | `#171B22` | `#F3F4F6` | the one raised surface: evidence and code live here |
| ink | `#F4F1E9` | `#101318` | ivory ink, 16.5:1 on ground |
| muted | `#8A93A3` | `#5A6271` | labels and captions, 6:1 |
| line | `#59616F` | `#C9CED6` | hairlines only, never text |
| evidence (accent) | `#79BFFF` | `#1F6FD1` | the brand accent: evidence, the square, the pointer trace |

Decision colors are a data encoding, not decoration. They appear only on a
verdict, always with the word and a shape, never on their own:

| Decision | Dark | Light | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| success (supported) | `#95D8AC` | `#1E7A47` | filled square |
| failure (refuted) | `#FF877A` | `#C2412F` | square with a cut (struck) |
| abstained (unknown) | `#F0C878` | `#8A5A00` | outlined square |

Core palette = ground + ink + one accent (R-29). The three decision colors are a
semantic scale bound to the SDK's three legal decisions, the same way a status
light is.

## Type

| Role | Family | Settings | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| display | Archivo (OFL) | wdth 112 to 125, wght 600 to 700, tracking -0.02em | wide and settled: the outcome voice |
| tension | Archivo | wdth 62, wght 800 | condensed: the unverified claim |
| text | Archivo | wdth 100, wght 400 to 500 | captions and UI text |
| data | Martian Mono (OFL) | wdth 100, wght 400 to 500 | pointers, values, stdout; one mono, chosen for its wide, legible zero and slash |

The width axis carries meaning: a claim is set condensed, an outcome wide. The
type relaxes into its final state when the verdict lands.

Film sizes at 1080p: headlines 84 to 100 px, body 40 to 46 px, code at least
30 px. No uppercase tracked labels; labels are lowercase Martian Mono at 26 px.

## Motion

- One ease family: `expo.out` for arrivals, `power2.inOut` for camera moves.
  Never `back` or `elastic`: verdicts do not bounce.
- Entrances 300 to 450 ms, staggers 60 to 80 ms, key messages hold at least 1.8 s.
- Big things move slower than small things.
- The pointer trace is drawn with SVG stroke-dashoffset and stops dead.
- Data and code stay still while they are read.
- Maximum two elements compete for attention at any time.

## Identity motif

The evidence square (S x S, accent color). It marks every piece of evidence on
screen, it is the bullet of the decision legend, and it closes every film by
docking under the n. It is never a decorative dot, never glows, never pulses.

## Avoided on purpose

Fake terminal chrome (traffic-light dots): stdout appears as a plain panel with
the command above it, labeled "real output". No background grid, no gradient
glow, no particles, no robots, no sparkles, no invented numbers, no customer
logos.

## Brand override record (R-37)

- The tagline "Verify the outcome, not the attempt." uses a "not X, Y"
  construction (negative parallelism). It is the owner's established tagline
  (PyPI, README, package metadata); kept as canon, flagged to the owner on
  2026-10-20.
